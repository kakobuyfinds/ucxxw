OopBuy buying guide

01 · Shortlist

Check OopBuy product links

Start with two or three comparable listings. For a Weidian product link, Taobao listing, or 1688 supplier page, confirm the original seller URL, exact color option, size availability, and seller return window.

Save the listing URL and the variant together. If you encounter a dead spreadsheet link, find a live listing in the same category and compare its material and measurements again.

02 · Inspect

Read OopBuy QC photos

For a sneaker QC check, look for both shoes, the outsole, and the size tag. Compare the insole length with a pair you wear. For a hoodie size chart, match flat chest width and sleeve length to a garment you own.

For a jersey fit check, compare chest and body length. For caps and jewelry, confirm head circumference or bracelet inner circumference. If a warehouse photo hides a clasp or seam, ask for a close-up where available.

03 · Compare

Estimate OopBuy shipping costs

Add the item price, domestic delivery fee, agent service fees, and the international shipping estimate. Check the packed parcel weight and dimensions, since a carrier may use volumetric weight when quoting a route.

For a consolidated parcel, compare the cost after packing. Check tracking, route restrictions, and potential import charges for your destination before selecting a service.

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OopBuy spreadsheet?
It is a category-based directory for finding and comparing product listings. The category links on this independent guide open HipoBuyFind.
What should I check before copying a product link?
Open the live listing and check the seller, selected size and color, current availability, and return terms. Copy the original listing URL when it is available.
Can QC photos verify authenticity?
QC photos can show visible condition, measurements, and some construction details. For authenticity, use verifiable seller records or a qualified authentication service.
How do I estimate the full shipping cost?
Use your destination and the packed weight and dimensions to compare available routes. Check service fees and any destination taxes or duties before paying.
